# Billing FAQ

## When am I charged?

Subscriptions renew on the same day each month (or year for annual plans). The invoice is emailed to the billing contact when the payment succeeds.

## Where can I find invoices?

Account owners and billing admins can download every invoice under **Settings → Billing → Invoices**. Invoices can be re-sent to a different email address from the same page.

## I was charged twice

Duplicate charges are usually a pending authorization that disappears within 3–5 business days. If both charges appear as completed on the card statement, collect the invoice numbers and last four digits of the card and escalate to BILLING. Confirmed duplicate charges are refunded in full.

## Refund policy

- Monthly plans: no refunds for partial months, but the plan can be cancelled at any time and stays active until the end of the billing period.
- Annual plans: a prorated refund is available within 30 days of renewal.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method and take 5–10 business days to appear.

## Changing plans

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date.

## Taxes and company details

VAT/GST numbers and company addresses can be added under **Settings → Billing → Company details**. Changes only apply to future invoices.
