# Account Cancellation Policy

## Who can cancel

Only the account owner can cancel a subscription. Other admins can see the cancel option but it is disabled for them.

## How to cancel

Go to **Settings → Billing → Cancel subscription**. The customer is asked for a reason; this is optional.

After cancelling:

- the workspace stays fully usable until the end of the current billing period,
- no further charges are made,
- the workspace becomes read-only after the period ends.

## Data retention

Data from cancelled workspaces is kept for 60 days after the subscription ends, so customers can reactivate without losing anything. After 60 days the data is permanently deleted.

Customers who need their data deleted immediately (for example under GDPR) must request it from the account owner's email address. These requests go to the SECURITY category and are completed within 30 days.

## Reactivating

Reactivation within 60 days restores all data. The customer chooses a plan and enters payment details as usual.
