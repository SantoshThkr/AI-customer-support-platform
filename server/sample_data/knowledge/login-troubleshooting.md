# Login Troubleshooting

Use this checklist when a customer cannot sign in.

## "Invalid email or password"

- Confirm the customer is using the correct email. Accounts are unique per email address.
- Ask them to reset the password (see the Password Reset Guide).
- Password managers often autofill an outdated password after a reset.

## Account locked

After five failed sign-in attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes. The lock clears automatically. Agents should not unlock accounts manually unless the customer has verified their identity.

## Two-factor authentication problems

- Codes from authenticator apps depend on the phone's clock. Ask the customer to enable automatic time on their device.
- Each backup code works once. If all backup codes are used, the account owner must contact support from the registered email address for identity verification.

## Single sign-on (SSO)

Workspaces on the Business plan can require SSO. Customers on these workspaces must use "Sign in with SSO". Errors that mention SAML or the identity provider should be escalated to the TECHNICAL queue with the exact error text and time.

## Browser issues

Blocked third-party cookies or strict tracking protection can break the sign-in redirect. Ask the customer to try a private window or another browser before escalating.
